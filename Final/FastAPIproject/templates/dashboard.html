Overview

Our platform connects farmers directly with markets, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair prices. We use AI to grade crops, provide market insights, and optimize supply chains. Farmers can track their crop quality, find the best markets, and receive real-time updates on pricing trends. Markets benefit from consistent quality produce and reliable supply. This initiative supports sustainable agriculture, local economies, and food security.

Tip: Click on a farmer or market on the map to see basic information. Double-click to view detailed information.
